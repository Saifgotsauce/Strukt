You see one name on the poster. Behind it, a club night is built by a crew most people never meet. Here is one night, taken apart piece by piece.

## The boss

Before a single ticket sells, a promoter bets on the night. They book the room, the DJs and the crew, and they carry the risk if nobody shows up.

## The site

Riggers and site crews turn an empty room or warehouse into a venue. Truss goes up, power gets run, and everything is checked before anyone else arrives.

## The sound

Sound engineers tune the system to the room so the low end hits without drowning the vocals. A great system in the wrong room sounds worse than a modest one tuned properly.

## The light

Lighting designers program every cue, often the night before. When the drop lands exactly as the room goes dark, that was planned.

## The stage

Stage managers call changeovers between sets and keep the night on time, so one DJ hands over to the next without dead air.

## The door, the bar and the safety

Door staff set the tone from the first second. Bar staff keep a full room going until close. Medics and safety crews make sure everyone gets home.

## The story

Photographers, writers and designers make sure the night is remembered, from the flyer that sold it to the photos everyone shares the next day.

## The floor

And then there's you. The crowd finishes it.
