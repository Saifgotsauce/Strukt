# Worked rewrites of STRUKT copy

Each example shows the problem, the rule it breaks, and a rewrite. Use these as calibration for the house voice.

---

**Standfirst band (Night desk)**

Before:
> Think about the last show that stopped you mid-sentence. You probably know who was playing. You probably don't know who made that moment.

Problems: reader-quiz opener; nobody stops mid-sentence in a club; two split sentences that read bleak and generated.

After:
> You'll always remember who was playing, but almost nobody knows who built the moment that made the whole room lose it.

---

**Pull quote (Night desk)**

Before:
> A scene isn't a lineup. It's the people who keep showing up between lineups.

Problem: the "isn't X, it's Y" reflex.

After:
> The lineup is one night. The scene is everyone who keeps coming back after it.

---

**Who STRUKT is for (Night desk)**

Before:
> If you've ever made a night happen and gone uncredited, this is for you.

Problem: true, but only speaks to people who already work nights. It leaves out artists and people who haven't been given a chance yet.

After:
> If you've ever made something for a night and watched it go uncredited, you belong here. If nobody has seen your work yet, you especially belong here.

---

**Kicker line (Night desk)**

Before:
> STRUKT exists to put them there.

Problem: "exists to" sounds like a mission statement.

After:
> This magazine is where their names go.

---

**Call to action (Signal desk)**

Before:
> Built something? Submit your work to STRUKT.

Problem: fine, but reads like a form button and leaves out artists.

After:
> Made something for the night? Send it to us.
