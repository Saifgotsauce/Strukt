## Asking for press photos

Send this to the Do LaB and to Shambhala:

> Hi, I'm writing from STRUKT, a magazine that credits everyone behind club nights and festivals. We've published a piece crediting the people who build [the Do LaB stage / Shambhala], and we'd love to run it with your approved press photos. Could you send a few, and tell us exactly how each photographer should be credited? We credit every photographer by name, and we'd be glad to add any crew names you'd like on the record too.
