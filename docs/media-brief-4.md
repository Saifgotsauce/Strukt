# STRUKT media brief 4: specific images for every post

The stock photos on the live posts set the mood. This brief replaces or adds images that show the actual places, people and things each section is about. It covers:

- **Part A:** the two live posts from bundle 2 (UK garage and live sound), which have mood photos but none of the specific people or venues.
- **Part B:** the four new posts in bundle 3, replacing the stand-in searches in media-brief-3.md.

## Rules (same as always)

- Wikimedia Commons: only CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Open each file page, copy the author and licence exactly, and credit as "Photo: Name, licence, via Wikimedia Commons". CC BY-SA images keep their licence named in the credit.
- "Infobox image" means the photo at the top right of the Wikipedia article. Click it, open "More details" to reach its Commons page, and check the licence there. If it isn't one of the allowed licences, skip it.
- Never use Getty, FilmMagic, Alamy, agency photos, Instagram images, or event posters.
- Press photos only with written permission, credited exactly as the artist or festival asks.
- Save as WebP (2000px wide for heroes, 1400px in-body), in public/images/journal/<post-slug>/, and record everything in src/data/media-credits.json.
- Keep the existing stock photos where the brief doesn't replace them.
