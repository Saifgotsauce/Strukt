## Asking for official press photos

Official press photos are the best option for MPH and for any artist without a licensed Commons photo. Labels and managers usually allow editorial use with a credit, but only with their permission. Send this to MPH's team at Black Book Records or his management, and adapt it for the others:

> Hi, I'm writing from STRUKT, a magazine about the people who build club nights and festivals. We've published a feature on UK garage in North America that covers MPH's work with Black Book Records and his Vancouver show on October 31. Could you send an approved press photo we can use with the piece, and tell us exactly how the photographer should be credited? We credit every photographer by name. Thanks.

Keep their written reply, and record it in src/data/media-credits.json as the licence ("Press photo, used with permission from [team], [date]").
