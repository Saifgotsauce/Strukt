A magazine for everyone who makes the night, from the person rigging the lights to the artist whose work hangs behind the booth. We take it apart so every one of them gets named.

### "Where it starts" section
Heading: Where it starts

Large opening line:
We're from Vancouver, a city that turns out in the thousands when a big name lands and goes quiet the week after.

Column 1:
That pattern isn't only ours. Every city has the kid teaching themselves a lighting desk on a borrowed laptop, the painter whose canvases never leave the bedroom, the designer making clothes nobody has photographed yet, and the rigger who could build a stage in a car park.

Column 2:
Most of them are working alone and waiting for a reason to find each other. We'd like to be that reason, and to build something that's still standing long after the tour bus pulls out.

### "Why STRUKT" section
Lede paragraph 1:
The name comes from structure. Every night out is built from parts, and every part has people in it: the sound, the light, the stage, the art on the walls, the clothes on the floor, the door, the bar, and the ones keeping everyone safe.

Lede paragraph 2:
We take the night apart the way you'd open up a machine to see how it runs, then we put every person back in the credits.

### "How it works" section
Every week we sit down with someone who builds the night. Some come up through the underground and some have lit the biggest stages on earth, and we run them side by side because the work is the same work.

Every night we cover gets a credits card with every person on it. Nobody gets filed under "and crew."

### "Going everywhere" section
A rigger in Lagos, a lighting designer in Berlin and a set painter in São Paulo pull the same hours and go just as unseen. We want them in the same room, even if the room is a magazine.

## Call for creatives page (src/pages/call-for-creatives.astro)

### Headline
Show us what you made.

### Intro under the headline
We're looking for the first people to feature in STRUKT. You don't need a big name, a festival résumé or a following, just work you're proud of.

### Button
Start your submission

### Steps band heading
What happens after you send it

Steps (unchanged wording except step 3):
1. You send the form. It takes about five minutes.
2. We look at every submission ourselves.
3. If it's a fit, we set up an interview however suits you: a video call, voice notes or in writing.
4. You see your feature before it goes live.
5. It's published, and you're credited and tagged.

### Violet band
Big line: Being featured is free, and it always will be.
Small line: Nobody pays to be in STRUKT. All we ask for is the work and the story behind it.

### Form intro (left column)
Heading: The form
Paragraph: Eight questions. Answer the way you'd talk to someone outside the venue after load-out. We care about the work, not the polish.

### Question 06 label and hint
Label: Tell us about something you made for a night
Hint: What was it, what did you do, and what went wrong that nobody noticed?

(When "Someone else" is selected, the label becomes: Tell us about something they made for a night)
